AI candlestick forecasting, running in your browser

This site brings Kronos, an open model trained on candles from more than 45 exchanges, to your own device. Pick a pair, press “Forecast” and the model draws dozens of possible futures. No account needed, and your queries never go through a server.

What it does

It is a tool to try a forecasting model on real data and check, with numbers, whether it is worth listening to.

Scenarios, not a single number
Kronos generates many possible paths. From them come the median, the 50 % and 80 % bands and the probability that the price ends higher.
Compared with what happened
You can forecast from a point in the past and see the error, whether it got the direction right and whether it beats assuming the price stays put. The backtest repeats this over many dates.
Data from four exchanges
Binance, Bybit, OKX and Coinbase, live and requested by your own browser. It also accepts a CSV with your own candles.
Five models
Kronos mini, small and base, plus Amazon’s Chronos-Bolt as a second opinion. You can pin a forecast and run another on top to compare them.
Everything on your device
The model is downloaded once (34 to 429 MB), stays in the browser and runs on the GPU, or on the CPU when WebGPU is not available.
Exportable
Forecasts export to CSV or JSON, the chart to PNG, and the settings can be shared with a link.

What is Kronos

A model that treats price candles as a language. Just as a text model learns which word comes next, Kronos learns which candle comes next.

It was published by Yu Shi, Zongliang Fu, Shuo Chen, Bohan Zhao, Wei Xu, Changshui Zhang and Jian Li in “Kronos: A Foundation Model for the Language of Financial Markets”, accepted at AAAI 2026. It was trained on data from more than 45 exchanges, and its code and weights are open (MIT). It works in three steps:

  1. Tokenizer. Turns each candle (open, high, low, close and volume) into two tokens: a coarse one with the overall shape and a fine one with the detail.
  2. Transformer. Reads the sequence of tokens and gives the probability of every possible next token. One is drawn at random according to those probabilities, appended, and the process repeats.
  3. Scenarios. Each repetition gives a different future. How much they differ is the model’s uncertainty.

Chronos-Bolt is a general-purpose model from Amazon (Apache-2.0). It only forecasts the close and does so in a single pass, so it is fast and useful as a check on Kronos.

How it works

Kronos is designed to run with Python and PyTorch on a server. To run in a browser, the same model is exported to ONNX and runs on ONNX Runtime Web. Checked with tests: its forecasts match the original's.

One token per step
The original code processes the whole context again for every new candle. Here the model is exported to ONNX with a key/value cache: the context is processed once, shared by every path, and each step computes a single token.
Checked against the original
The tests run the official Kronos in PyTorch and the browser engine on the same candles. Without randomness they give the same forecast; internal outputs differ by less than 0.00004.
GPU or CPU
With WebGPU the cache stays on the graphics card between steps. Without WebGPU, the same model runs with multi-threaded WebAssembly.

Limits

It is not a buy or sell signal, nor financial advice.

No model forecasts markets reliably, and Kronos is often wrong. That is why the app measures every forecast against the simplest option, assuming the price does not move. If the backtest does not beat it, the model adds nothing for that market and interval.

Large models are slow on modest computers and phones. Kronos-large is not available because its weights are not public.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, with no accounts or ads. Your device does the computing, so running it costs nobody else anything.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. Your browser requests the candles from the exchange and the model runs on your device; this site only serves files. Details are in the privacy policy.

Which browser do I need?

Any current one. With WebGPU (desktop Chrome and Edge, among others) it uses the graphics card; without it, it uses the CPU, somewhat slower. Small models work well on phones.

How long does it take?

Kronos-mini forecasts 32 candles with 12 paths in a few seconds to half a minute, depending on the device. Larger models take much longer. The download only happens the first time.

Can I use my own data?

Yes: a CSV with date, open, high, low and close, plus volume if you have it. It works for stocks, currencies or any candlestick series. The file never leaves your device.

Can I run it myself?

The repository has the app, the scripts that convert the official weights to ONNX and the tests that compare them with PyTorch. It can be hosted on any static file server.
