What this is

              

                A tool to run time-series forecasting models in your own browser. Model weights are downloaded once and kept in the browser cache; candles are requested
                directly from the chosen exchange. No data goes through an intermediate server.
              

              
Models

              
    
                
  • 
                  Kronos (MIT): a foundation model trained on OHLCV candles from more than 45 markets. It
                  generates full candles by sampling several possible paths; the median and the bands come from them.
                
    • 
                
  • 
                  Chronos-Bolt (Apache-2.0): a general-purpose model from Amazon. It forecasts
                  the close only, in one pass, as quantiles from 10 % to 90 %. Useful as a second opinion.
                
    • 
              

              
Reading the chart

              
    
                
  • The blue line is the forecast median; the bands are the ranges between percentiles (half and 80 % of the scenarios).
    • 
                
  • Hollow candles are the mean of Kronos’ paths. The thin lines are individual paths.
    • 
                
  • With “Candles back” above 0, the forecast starts in the past and is compared with what happened next.
    • 
              

              
Warning

              

                Markets cannot be forecast reliably. These outputs are statistical scenarios from a model, not investment advice. Check the backtest before trusting a forecast: a skill close to 0
                means the model does no better than assuming the price stays put.
